Technology Clubs

Technology clubs focus on programming, artificial intelligence, web development, and robotics. These clubs help students gain practical skills and work on real-world projects.

Sports Clubs

Sports clubs promote physical fitness and teamwork through activities like football, cricket, basketball, and athletics. Students can participate in competitions and tournaments.

Cultural Clubs

Cultural clubs celebrate diversity through music, dance, art, and drama. They organize events that showcase traditions and creativity from different cultures.

Debate Societies

Debate societies improve public speaking, critical thinking, and argumentation skills. Members participate in debates, discussions, and competitions.
